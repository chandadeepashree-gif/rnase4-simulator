How it works: the tube shows five RNA molecules; the percentages, curves, gel and bars come from a full simulation of 200 molecules with RNase 4 added in proportion to the dose. RNA is cut only when an enzyme bumps into it, at the U/A or U/G site (25 + 17 nt fragments). IL ions build a shield around each RNA (teal glow, stronger at higher concentration and for better-protecting ILs; ion shapes show C8–C12 fatty-acid tails, arginate + charge, SNAC rings); when the shield blocks an enzyme it bounces off (teal flash), and a cut RNA loses its shield. How many cuts can happen by each moment follows the measured gel data, so every run ends on the measured values (red dashes / points). A teaching model, not a kinetic fit.
